About Commodity Hub

Commodity market data, analytics, and news

What we do

Commodity Hub tracks 31 commodities across energy, metals, grains, livestock, dairy, and industrials, sourced from CME, CBOT, COMEX, and NYMEX. Alongside live prices, the app provides the analysis tools commodity traders actually use — forward curves and term structure, Commitment of Traders positioning, historical seasonality, spread monitoring, portfolio and risk tools, and an AI copilot for market questions.

The "Trade" section links to independent, regulated brokers for people who want to act on what they're tracking — see our Terms of Service for how that works and what we do (and don't) do in that relationship.

How we source data

Prices and market data come from licensed third-party providers — Massive Futures for CME/CBOT/COMEX/NYMEX quotes, curves, and history, with FMP covering select ICE/LME instruments. Data may be delayed and is provided for informational purposes; see our Terms of Service for the full disclaimer.

Get it

Commodity Hub is available as a web app at app.commodity-hub.eu, with a native Android app in progress. The free tier covers the core commodity catalog with no card required; Premium and Pro tiers add alerts, exports, and advanced analytics — see pricing.
